Retail sales · 2020–2021 · 6 PAGES

Food n Stuff

I compared 2020 and 2021 retail sales, then broke the change down by category, division, store, and product. Hobbies and the Mountain division stood out as areas that needed a closer look.

What I recommended

I recommended reviewing stores individually, comparing strategies across divisions, and looking at complementary products. These were classroom recommendations; I did not implement them or measure their results.

Sports performance and team economics · 8 PAGES

NBA Stats 2022

I compared player effectiveness and playing time by draft round, experience, age, and position. I also looked at team salary relative to wins and compared players who attended college with those who did not.

How to read the comparisons

Players active in 2022 and players drafted in 2022 are different groups, so I gave them separate comparisons. Playing time and group size matter when reading these charts. The comparisons alone cannot explain what caused a performance difference.

Audience reach and content patterns · 7 PAGES

TEDx

I looked at the most-viewed talks and compared their length, topics, publication month, and time online. The report explores what those talks had in common and how views changed over time.

What the patterns suggest

I used the patterns to suggest topics and release timing worth exploring. A popular topic or publication month in this dataset does not mean a future talk will get more views.
