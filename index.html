SUPPLY CHAIN DECISION SUPPORT · REVELYST INTERNSHIP

Shipping Optimization Under Tariff Pressure

When European inventory releases stalled after new tariffs were announced, operations needed a fast way to understand the orders, pallet requirements, and financial tradeoffs between air and sea freight. I served as the primary owner of the solution until the end of my internship.

  • Owned stakeholder discovery, requirements, workbook design, logic, validation, rollout, support, and ongoing maintenance.
  • Delivered a usable Excel solution within two days for a nontechnical audience.
  • Assigned pallets to orders and tracked product composition, weight, and volume.
  • Compared freight methods using cost-efficiency and transit-time inputs.
  • Added validation against pallet, weight, volume, and shipping-capacity limits.
WHY EXCEL WAS THE RIGHT ANSWER

The team already knew Excel and needed something they could use quickly. It let me build the checks into the workbook and make updates without asking users to learn another tool.

ADOPTION SIGNAL

Months later, a dealer-experience teammate asked whether I had built the tool and requested more products be added. The team was still using it.

EXCEL · GAMING COMMUNITY

Warcraft III Risk: Free-for-All Tournament Data

Risk is a Warcraft III custom map that combines real-time strategy gameplay with a map of Europe. Our small group tracked tournament games and player results. I developed analytical elements that were used in YouTube videos reaching hundreds of people who followed the tournament scene.

The game recordsGame IDs, dates, video links, winners, game duration, and player counts.
The player comparisonsWins, games played, win rate, average placement, kills, deaths, and kill/death ratios, with a date cutoff for the summaries.
The rating experimentA custom weighted score labeled “ELO” in the workbook, combining win rate, placement, kill/death ratio, and rival counts.
My contributionDeveloping analytical elements for a shared hobby project and its community videos.

From game results to community videos

The workbook links individual game results to player summaries using lookup and conditional aggregation formulas. It gave us a way to compare players across games and bring statistics into videos for people already familiar with the tournament scene.
