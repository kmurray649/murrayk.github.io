Warcraft III Risk: Free-for-All Tournament Data

Risk is a Warcraft III custom map that combines real-time strategy gameplay with a map of Europe. Our small group tracked tournament games and player results. I handled data ingestion and set up a local database so I could use SQL to manipulate and analyze the tournament data. I also built the analytics shared in YouTube videos reaching hundreds of people who followed the tournament scene.

The game recordsGame IDs, dates, video links, winners, game duration, and player counts.
The player comparisonsWins, games played, win rate, average placement, kills, deaths, and kill/death ratios, with a date cutoff for the summaries.
The rating experimentA custom weighted score labeled “ELO” in the workbook, combining win rate, placement, kill/death ratio, and rival counts.
My contributionData ingestion, local database setup, SQL data manipulation, and building the analytics shared in community YouTube videos.

My part in the project

I took care of getting the tournament data into a local database and used SQL to manipulate it and do analytical work. I also built the analytics that were shared in the group’s YouTube videos.

The shared workbook links individual game results to player summaries using lookup and conditional aggregation formulas. It gave the group a way to compare players across games.
